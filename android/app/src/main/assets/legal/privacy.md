# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 April 2025

## 1. Introduction

Prompt-Gineer respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. It applies to the Android application and the website.

The Android app is local-first: drafts, the autosaved workspace, coach transcripts, bookmarks and cached feeds live in a private database on your device. Nothing is uploaded until you save to your account, publish publicly, or ask for an AI generation.

## 2. Data We Collect

- **Identity data** — display name and avatar URL, if you provide them.
- **Contact data** — your email address.
- **Account data** — a password hash (never the password itself), role and account preferences such as your learning opt-in.
- **Content data** — prompts, templates, sections, comments, likes and bookmarks that you choose to store or publish.
- **Technical data** — IP address and basic request metadata seen by the server when the app calls the API.
- **Usage data** — daily AI generation counts used to enforce the free-tier limit.
- **Provider keys** — if you connect your own AI provider, the key is encrypted before it is stored and is never returned to the app.

We do not run third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the Android app. Ads shown in the app are editorial placements managed by the service and are clearly marked.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use data to operate the contract between us and you: authenticating you, syncing your library, publishing what you ask us to publish, crediting points, enforcing quotas, and responding to support requests.

We also use it for legitimate interests such as keeping the service secure, preventing abuse and improving prompt guidance. When you opt in to learning, public prompts may inform those improvements. Private prompts are excluded, and you can change the setting in your profile at any time.

## 4. AI Providers

When you ask for an AI generation, the prompt or message history you submit is sent through our API to the selected provider (for example, the platform's NVIDIA NIM deployment, or a provider you connected). Their handling of that text is governed by their terms. If you use your own key, the request is routed to that provider directly through our gateway and the key is decrypted only for the duration of the call.

## 5. Data Sharing

We share data only with:

- infrastructure providers that host the API and database;
- professional advisers where legally required;
- regulators or authorities when the law requires it;
- a successor entity if the service is sold or merged, under the same commitments.

We do not sell personal data.

## 6. Data Security

Passwords are hashed, provider keys are encrypted, and transport uses HTTPS. Access to production data is limited to people who need it. If a breach occurs, we will notify you and any regulator where the law requires it.

## 7. Data Retention

Account data and content are kept while your account exists. Deleting your account deletes your profile, prompts, templates, comments and connected keys. Server logs and backups may persist for a short period for security and recovery purposes.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data, and to withdraw consent.

Inside the app you can:

- export a JSON copy of your data from Settings;
- correct your profile from the Profile screen;
- delete your account from Settings, which removes your data.

## 9. Local Storage

The app stores your session token in Android's encrypted shared preferences where available, and settings, drafts, bookmarks and cached feeds in private app storage. Clearing local data in Settings removes drafts, bookmarks, the autosaved workspace and caches from the device without affecting published prompts.

We do not use cookies in the Android app because there is no browser engine involved.

## 10. Children

The service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the service after that means you accept the updated policy.

## 12. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the Contact screen in the app.
