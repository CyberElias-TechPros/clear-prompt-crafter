# How Prompt-Gineer works

## The idea

Most bad AI answers start with a vague request. Prompt-Gineer turns a fuzzy idea into a brief a model can act on, then shows you why it works.

## Three ways to build

**Structured.** Fill in context, task, guidelines and constraints. Add optional sections for edge cases, security and performance when they matter. The preview assembles itself, so what you copy is exactly what you reviewed.

**Conversational.** Describe what you need in plain language. The coach asks one useful question at a time and drafts a prompt as you go. Without a connection or an account, an on-device coach keeps the conversation moving.

**Meta-prompting.** Paste an existing prompt. The CLEAR review scores it on five criteria and returns specific fixes plus an improved version you can edit before using.

## The CLEAR framework

- **C — Concise.** Every sentence earns its place. Reduce filler and repetition.
- **L — Logical.** Sections follow an order a model can follow: context, task, rules, limits.
- **E — Explicit.** Say what good looks like: format, length, audience, tone.
- **A — Adaptive.** Leave room for the model to ask, choose or adapt when information is missing.
- **R — Reflective.** Ask for checks, uncertainty, or a self-review so errors surface early.

## Points and badges

Publishing a prompt earns 10 points. Receiving a like earns 2, and writing a comment earns 2. Points and badges appear on the leaderboard. They exist to reward contributing something useful, not to reward volume for its own sake.

## AI, optionally

The app works without AI. The review engine and coach heuristics run on-device. When you want a model in the loop, use the free platform tier or connect your own provider key. Keys are encrypted server-side and never returned to the app.

## Your data

Drafts and bookmarks live on your device. You choose what to publish. You can export a JSON copy of your account data or delete your account from Settings.
